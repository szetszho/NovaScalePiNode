Usual weight lets new weigh-ins be matched to this person before they have any history. Height, birth date and sex are needed for BMI and body composition, and for sending this person's profile to the scale.

Which published equations the model uses. General: NHANES III, BMI 25 / 30. East Asian (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and similar): Chinese-adult equations, WHO Asia BMI 23 / 27.5.

Both are kept for every weigh-in and shown side by side on the Body tab. The scale's analysis only exists for the person set as the scale profile (Settings).
